Your University Journey & Checklist

University Life starts with more than subject Knowledge

The move from school to university brings new expectations, greater independance and more personal responsibility. This programme is designed to help school leavers build practical, interpersonal and academic ready skills that make that transition stronger.

THE SKILLS GAP WE HELP YOU CLOSE
  • Confidence and Communication
    • Speak with greater impact and clarity, participate actively and communicate appropriately in academic settings.
  • Presentation Skills
    • Structure ideas, present with purpose and become more comfortable communicating to an audience.
  • Digital Literacy & AI
    • Use Digital tools and applications responsibly to support learning, productivity and everyday problem solving.
  • Independent Learning
    • Develop self direction, time awareness, growth mindset and practical habits for university study.

YOUR OUTCOMES

What you will build in three weeks.

The programme is competency-focused: every part of the experience is designed around practical capabilities you can carry into university, work and lifelong learning.

01
Self-awareness & Direction
Identify your strengths, learning style, values and goals for personal development.
02
Professional Communication
Build appropriate communication, professional behaviour and time-management habits.
03
Teamwork & Collaboration
Contribute respectfully, communicate effectively and work productively with others.
04
Problem-solving & Mindset
Apply basic problem-solving methods and growth-mindset strategies to real situations.
05
Digital & AI Fluency
Use digital tools and AI applications responsibly for learning and productivity.
06
A personal Action Plan
Create a practical plan for your future academic, career and lifelong-learning goals.
